Startup ordering is health-gated: the control plane applies Postgres migrations and
the consumer applies the ClickHouse schema on startup, and dependents wait on their
readiness via init containers. Give the stack a minute on first install.

Smoke test the ingest path:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "prism.fullname" $ }}-controlplane 8080:8080 &
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "prism.fullname" $ }}-gateway 8090:8090 &

Then create a tenant and an API key against localhost:8080, and post OTLP metrics to
localhost:8090/v1/metrics. See docs/DEPLOYMENT.md for the full walkthrough.
